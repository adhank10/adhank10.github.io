About

I hold an MSc in Computing with a specialization in Artificial Intelligence from Dublin City University, where I graduated with First Class Honours.

My MSc research focused on automatic news summarization. Since then, my work has expanded into machine learning, natural language processing, retrieval-augmented generation, large language models and autonomous AI systems.

I am currently a Visiting Faculty member at YMCA University of Science and Technology, where I teach Machine Learning, Data Warehousing and Data Mining, Embedded Systems and Problem Solving, while supervising student projects in AI and related areas.
