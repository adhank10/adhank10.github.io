Research

MSc Research Project · Dublin City University · 2022–2023

Automatic News Summarization

A comparative study of five extractive and abstractive text-summarization approaches using the CNN/DailyMail dataset: TF–IDF, graph-based ranking, a hybrid machine-learning approach, Seq2Seq and PEGASUS.

The research included a graph-based extractive method using TF–IDF sentence representations, cosine similarity and PageRank-based sentence ranking; a hybrid approach using a Random Forest regressor; a Seq2Seq architecture with a bidirectional LSTM encoder and LSTM decoder; and fine-tuning of PEGASUS for news summarization.

The evaluated approaches were compared using ROUGE-1, ROUGE-2 and ROUGE-L, and the strongest approaches were integrated into a Flask-based application for testing on live news articles.
